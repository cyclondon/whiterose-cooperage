Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about our cask inventory, shipping logistics, or private investments? Find detailed answers below.

What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

We maintain no minimum order quantity (MOQ) restrictions on our cask sourcing orders. Whether you need a single 40L micro-barrel or full container loads, we accommodate your exact scale.

How are your casks shipped internationally?

We coordinate secure global logistics from our warehousing hubs directly to distilleries across the UK, Europe, Asia, and the Americas.

Can I invest in private casks or secure custom bottlings?

Yes. Through our Private Cask Allocations program, collectors and partners can acquire rare assets like our Monlanchum series for long-term appreciation or bespoke label bottling.
