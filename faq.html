FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers about cask sourcing, exclusive bottling and cask investment.

Cask Sourcing

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ) for oak casks?

We have no minimum order quantity. Whether you need a single 40L experimental barrel or full container loads, we scale to your production needs.

What types of wood and cask finishes do you offer?

Beyond standard American white oak (Quercus alba) and European oak (Quercus robur), we specialise in rare hybrid combinations — such as American oak staves with Mizunara, Sugi or Hinoki — alongside seasoned casks including our 20-year-old Pu-erh tea cask finishes. See our current inventory.

Can I order custom sizes, char levels or toast profiles?

Yes. Through our cooperage partners we offer bespoke dimensions, grain selection, toast and char levels, and specialty seasoning, tailored to accelerate or refine micro-batch maturation.

How does international shipping work?

We coordinate secure, climate-controlled logistics from our partner cooperages and storage hubs, using food-grade protective crating, to distilleries across the UK, Europe, Asia and the Americas.

Exclusive Bottling

What is White Rose Cooperage Exclusive Bottling?

Our exclusive bottling line is our own stamp of approval. We source exceptional, rare spirits globally and evaluate their character carefully before releasing them under our independent label.

How do you select spirits for your releases?

We search for unique, high-potential batches across the spirits world. Only expressions that meet our standards for wood interaction, flavour complexity and maturation depth earn the White Rose Cooperage label.

Can I collaborate with you on a bottling?

Yes. We work with distilleries, independent blenders, brand owners and private collectors, and can help with cask selection, custom seasoning and packaging compliance. Email us to discuss your idea.

Are your releases available to buy?

Yes. Our single-cask and small-batch bottlings are offered to collectors, connoisseurs and trade partners. Contact us for current availability.

Cask Investment

What is a private cask allocation?

It is the purchase of a physical cask of maturing spirit. You own the cask, which is stored in bond while it matures; you can hold it for the long term, bottle it under your own label, or sell it.

What are the risks of cask investment?

The value of a cask can fall as well as rise, casks can take time to sell, and returns are not guaranteed. Past performance of the whisky market is not a reliable guide to the future. Nothing on this website is financial advice, and we recommend seeking independent professional advice before buying a cask as an investment.

How are investment casks stored and insured?

Casks are held in bonded warehouses with controlled temperature and humidity, and are insured while in storage. Ask us for the storage and insurance terms that apply to a specific allocation.

How do I get started?

Contact our advisory team to review available single-cask allocations, expected maturation timelines and bonded storage transfer documentation.

What can I do with my cask at maturity?

You can bottle the spirit under a custom label, sell it through our trade channels, or transfer ownership of the physical cask.
