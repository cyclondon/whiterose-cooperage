Frequently Asked Questions

Explore detailed answers regarding our three core offerings: Cask Sourcing, Exclusive Bottling, and Cask Investment.

Cask Sourcing

What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) for oak casks?

We maintain strict no minimum order quantity policies. Whether you need a single 40L micro-experimental barrel or full container loads, we scale seamlessly to your production needs.

What types of wood and custom cask finishes do you offer?

Beyond standard American White Oak and European oak, we specialize in rare hybrid wood combinations—such as Mizunara and American White Oak staves—alongside innovative seasoned casks like our 20-year Pu-erh tea cask finishes.

How does international shipping and logistics work for distillery orders?

We coordinate secure, climate-controlled global logistics directly from our storage hubs using food-grade protective crating, delivering safely to distilleries across the UK, Europe, Asia, and the Americas.

Can craft distilleries order custom-sized barrels for micro-batch releases?

Yes, we support craft distilleries with bespoke dimensions and specialized char levels tailored specifically to accelerate or nuance micro-batch maturation profiles.

Exclusive Bottling

What is the Exclusive Bottling concept at White Rose Cooperage?

Our exclusive bottling line represents our own stamp of approval. We actively source and discover exceptional, rare spirits globally, carefully evaluating their character before releasing them under our own independent label.

How do you select the spirits for your independent releases?

Our team hunts down unique, high-potential batches across the spirits world. Only expressions that meet our rigorous standards for wood interaction, flavor complexity, and maturation depth earn the White Rose Cooperage label.

Are these exclusive releases available for general purchase or investment?

Yes, our curated single-cask and small-batch independent bottlings are made available to collectors, connoisseurs, and trade partners who appreciate meticulously vetted, rare expressions.

Cask Investment

Is whisky and spirit cask investment a secure alternative asset class?

Yes. Acquiring rare physical casks through our Private Cask Allocations program allows collectors and investors to hold tangible, maturing assets with strong historical long-term appreciation potential.

How are private casks stored and insured during the investment period?

All investment casks are securely warehoused in bonded, temperature-regulated facilities with full comprehensive asset insurance until you choose to bottle, sell, or transfer ownership.

Can I liquidate or bottle my investment cask at maturity?

Absolutely. When your cask reaches its optimal maturation peak, you have complete flexibility to bottle the spirit under a custom label, sell it through our trade channels, or trade the physical cask.
