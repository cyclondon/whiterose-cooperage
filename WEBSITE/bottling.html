Inaugural Release

Monlanchum (無量川)

Best Chinese Whisky - HK Best Spirits Awards 2024

Born from the legendary Wuliang Mountains (無量山) revered for their ancient Pu-erh trees and historic tea gardens, Monlanchum (無量川) stands as a tribute to an extraordinary heritage.

We are proud to partner with Monlanchum for our inaugural White Rose Cooperage Select range, featuring a strictly limited run of 2,024 bottles—a deliberate nod to the year they claimed their prestigious award.

Through a pioneering, global-first cask-seasoning technique utilizing rare, 20-year-aged Pu-erh tea casks, this expression transcends traditional boundaries, delivering remarkable depth and nuanced complexity.
