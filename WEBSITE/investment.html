The Monlanchum Acquisition

Strategic Cask Partnership

At White Rose Cooperage, our investment philosophy centers on the acquisition of rare, high-provenance casks that define the future of premium spirits. We bridge traditional cooperage with tangible, long-term asset value for our private partners.

We are proud to present our premier investment opportunity: the strategic procurement and allocation of authentic Monlanchum (無量川) casks. Celebrated for their award-winning 20-year-old Pu-erh tea cask seasoning technique, this offering provides private partners unique access to a hard asset merging profound cultural heritage with exceptional market scarcity.
