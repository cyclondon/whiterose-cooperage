Cask Sourcing & Global Supply

At White Rose Cooperage, cask sourcing serves as the bedrock of our global operations. We work alongside premier international partner cooperages to procure, season, and dispatch top-tier wooden casks to distilleries and craft producers worldwide.

No Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)

We believe craft distillation should be unconstrained by rigid commercial barriers. Whether you require a single experimental barrel for a micro-batch release or full container shipments for a commercial distillery, we maintain no minimum order quantity restrictions on our sourcing orders.

Our Cask Portfolio

  • American White Oak Ex-Bourbon Barrels: Sourced directly from premier Kentucky distilleries, ideal for foundational spirit maturation.
  • European Oak Sherry Butts & Puncheons: Seasoned with Oloroso, Pedro Ximénez, or Amontillado sherry to impart rich dried fruit and tannin complexity.
  • Specialty Infusion & Wine Casks: Including port pipes, sauternes barriques, and innovative specialty seasoning barrels like our signature tea-seasoned oak casks.
  • Custom Wood Specs & Toast Levels: Tailored grain selections (Quercus alba / Quercus robur) with precise light, medium, or heavy char and toast curves.
