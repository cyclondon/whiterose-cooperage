Cask Sourcing

Oak cask sourcing and global supply

We work with leading partner cooperages worldwide to source, season and ship quality casks to distilleries and craft producers — with no minimum order quantity.

No minimum order quantity

Whether you need one experimental barrel for a micro-batch or full container shipments for a commercial distillery, there is no minimum order.

Bespoke specifications

Custom dimensions, grain selection (Quercus alba / Quercus robur), toast and char levels, and specialty seasoning such as our tea-seasoned casks.

Global logistics

Secure, climate-controlled shipping in food-grade protective crating to distilleries across the UK, Europe, Asia and the Americas.
