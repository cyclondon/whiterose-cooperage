Flagship Release

Monlanchum (無量川)

Hong Kong Spirits Awards 2024 - Best Chinese Whisky

Born from the legendary Wuluo Mountain, celebrated for its ancient Pu-erh tea trees and historical plantations, "Monlanchum" pays homage to this extraordinary heritage.

We are honored to partner with Monlanchum to release the inaugural White Rose Cooperage Select series, strictly limited to 2,024 bottles—a deliberate nod to their award-winning vintage year.

Utilizing a pioneering world-first cask-seasoning technique featuring rare 20-year-old Pu-erh tea casks, this expression pushes traditional boundaries, delivering astonishing depth and intricate complexity.
