Exclusive releases

Rare spirits, carefully selected and released under the White Rose Cooperage label.

Flagship release

Monlanchum (無量川)

Hong Kong Spirits Awards 2024 – Best Chinese Whisky

Monlanchum takes its name from Wuliang Mountain in Yunnan, renowned for its ancient Pu-erh tea trees and historic plantations.

We are honoured to partner with Monlanchum on the first release in our White Rose Cooperage Select series, strictly limited to 2024 bottles — a nod to the year of its award.

The spirit is finished in rare 20-year-old Pu-erh tea-seasoned casks, a pioneering seasoning technique that gives remarkable depth and layered, earthy complexity.
