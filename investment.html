Cask Investment

Private cask allocations

Acquire exceptional, limited casks — to hold for the long term or to bottle under your own private label.

How it works

From allocation to maturity

  1. Review allocations

    Our advisory team shares available single-cask allocations, their provenance and expected maturation timelines.

  2. Acquire your cask

    Ownership is transferred to you with bonded storage transfer documentation.

  3. Mature in bond

    Your cask matures in a bonded warehouse with controlled temperature and humidity, insured while in storage.

  4. Choose your exit

    At maturity, bottle under a custom label, sell through our trade channels, or transfer the cask.
