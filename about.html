About White Rose Cooperage

White Rose Cooperage operates at the intersection of traditional woodworking craftsmanship and modern spirit curation. With operational hubs in Hong Kong and London, we serve distilleries, independent bottlers, and private asset collectors globally.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ) for cask sourcing?

We maintain a strict no minimum order quantity (MOQ) policy. Whether you are sourcing a single experimental barrel for a micro-batch release or full container shipments for a commercial distillery, we accommodate orders of all scales.

2. How does the exclusive bottling collaboration work?

We partner directly with distilleries, independent blenders, brand owners, and private collectors. We invite you to reach out directly to discuss your vision, and our team will assist with barrel selection, custom seasoning, and packaging compliance.

3. Where are investment casks stored?

All client and investment casks are securely stored in fully insured, bonded warehouses that maintain optimal temperature and humidity conditions for long-term wood maturation.

4. Can I customize the char levels and wood seasoning?

Yes. Through our cooperage partnerships, we offer bespoke wood specifications, including specific grain selections (Quercus alba / Quercus robur), toast levels, and unique infusion treatments like our specialty tea-seasoned casks.

5. How do I initiate a cask investment portfolio?

Potential investors can contact our advisory team to review available rare single-cask allocations, expected maturation timelines, and bonded storage transfer documentation.

6. What regions do you ship to?

We coordinate global logistics and secure international shipping from our partner cooperages and bonded facilities directly to your designated destination or warehouse.
