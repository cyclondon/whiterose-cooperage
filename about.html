About

About White Rose Cooperage

We work where traditional cooperage craft meets modern spirit curation. From hubs in Hong Kong and London, we serve distilleries, independent bottlers and private collectors worldwide.

What we do

Cask Sourcing is the foundation of our business. We work with leading partner cooperages to source, season and ship casks — from ex-bourbon barrels and sherry butts to Mizunara hybrids and tea-seasoned casks — with no minimum order quantity.

Exclusive Bottling is our own stamp of approval. We seek out exceptional spirits and release them under the White Rose Cooperage label, beginning with Monlanchum, and we collaborate with distilleries, brands and collectors on their own bottlings.

Cask Investment gives private collectors access to rare casks of exceptional provenance, matured in bonded warehouses and ready to hold, bottle or sell when the time is right.

Where we work

Our teams are based in Hong Kong and London, serving clients across Asia and Europe. Through our partner cooperages and bonded facilities, we coordinate shipping to distilleries in the UK, Europe, Asia and the Americas.

How we work

  • Flexible: no minimum order, from a single 40L barrel to full containers.
  • Bespoke: custom dimensions, grain, toast, char and seasoning through our cooperage partners.
  • Selective: only spirits that meet our standards for wood interaction, complexity and maturation carry our label.

Responsible drinking
